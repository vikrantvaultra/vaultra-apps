🛡️ Safety rails

  • Never auto-pays without a max price. Order total must be ≤ max × qty + fee allowance.
  • Never types OTP, CVV, card or UPI PIN. If the bank asks, it stops and pings you.
  • Only uses Buy Now — your existing cart is never checked out.
  • One checkout per store at a time; an item can't be ordered twice.
  • Daily budget caps everything auto-ordered today.
  • Auto-pay is off by default: it stops at the final button for you.
